# Accessibility Audit

I ran my site through [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) to check accessibility.

## What it found

The contrast between my link text and the background wasn't high enough, making it harder to read. It flagged a few links in the Intro section and a line in the Education section.

## What I changed

I made the link color darker so it's easier to read against the background. I also noticed the nav links had the same problem when you hover over them, so I changed the hover color too.
